Robert Villos, affectionately known as Yoyong or Yongvils among his family and close friends, moved to New Zealand in 2015. Being far from home strengthened his faith and deepened his devotion to the Santo Niño. What began as a quiet and personal devotion gradually evolved into a more active and visible expression of faith, as the Santo Niño mysteriously guided his spiritual journey.
Together with his wife, Jewel, Robert had the honor of serving as the 2023 Hermano and Hermana, reflecting their commitment and dedication to their faith community.
Although he holds a degree in Business, Yoyong chose to build his career in New Zealand's trades industry, specializing in group home construction projects. He currently serves as the Branch Manager of Mico Bathrooms and Plumbing Wairau, a division of Fletcher Building.
Yoyong and Jewel are proud and active members of the Couples for Christ West Auckland Chapter, where they continue to live out their faith through service, fellowship, and community involvement.
