Maria Consuelo “Chichi” Abadingo coordinates the community and choir groups involved in the annual Nine-Day Novena as part of her duties on the Liturgical Committee. She manages the Sinulog NZ social media pages and maintains the organization's website, ensuring that community members stay informed about the Trust's activities and events.

With more than 30 years of experience in business software development and project management, Chichi brings a wealth of professional expertise to her volunteer and community work. In her professional life, she works as an ERP Business Analyst. Outside of work, she enjoys writing, attending dance and fitness classes, and serving on the Holy Cross Papatoetoe Parish Liturgy Committee and organizes the monthly Filipino masses at Holy Cross.

Chichi also serves as Deputy Chair of Agape Branches, an organization that distributes rescued food to families in need. In addition, she is a member of the Le Malaga Trust, which supports caregivers of people living with dementia through community-led awareness and education initiatives. Research from the University of Auckland indicates that three New Zealanders develop dementia every hour, equivalent to approximately 500 people each week. Through her involvement with Le Malaga Trust, Chichi contributes to raising awareness of this growing health challenge and supporting carers.
