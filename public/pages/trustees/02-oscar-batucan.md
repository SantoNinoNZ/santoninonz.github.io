Sir Oscar Batucan exemplifies the qualities of a true servant leader. Throughout his distinguished career and lifelong commitment to community service, he has consistently dedicated himself to empowering others and creating positive change. Known for his exceptional communication skills, ability to build and inspire teams, innovative thinking, and effective problem-solving, Sir Oscar is widely respected for both his professional excellence and personal integrity.
A graduate of Bachelor of Commerce, Sir Oscar has made significant contributions across government service, community development, employment advocacy, and faith-based organizations. His career and community involvement have been marked by a steadfast commitment to serving others and uplifting diverse communities.
Highlights of his professional and community service include:
-Senior Executive Officer, Department of Social Welfare (South Auckland District)
-Manager, Inland Revenue Department (IRD), Child Support Agency, South Auckland
-Case Manager, Ministry of Social Development (Work and Income New Zealand)
-Justice of the Peace since 1994
-Project Development Manager
-Employment, Education, and Enterprise Consultant specializing in job placement and community development
-Active advocate for youth development and migrant support initiatives
-Radio Anchor, Filipinas Mabuhay on 104.6 Planet FM
Honors and Recognitions:
-Recipient of the Papal Award and Pope Benedict XVI Benemerenti Medal
-Invested as a Knight of the Pontifical Equestrian Order of St. Sylvester Pope and Martyr
-Recipient of the Queen's Service Medal (QSM) in the 2015 Honors List for outstanding community service
Through his unwavering dedication to faith, public service, and community development, Sir Oscar continues to inspire countless individuals and remains a respected leader, mentor, and advocate for positive social change.
