- Men's Open, 38A, and 45A
- Women's 15u

- Email payment proof to: alexandalest101@gmail.com

- **Venue:** North Shore EventFinda Stadium, 17 Silverfield Lane Glenfield, Wairau, Auckland

**Team Registration:**

- Teams will be included in the tournament after they satisfy these registration requirements:
    1. Full registration fees
    2. Team line up/roster submitted (no changes will be accepted after the due date)
    3. Acknowledgement or confirmation from organizers
- First teams to complete the registration requirements will be served first. Email your team age category with complete line up and jersey number ASAP to: alexandalest101@gmail.com
- Team line-up shall be submitted on or before 19 Dec 2025.
- Teams are allowed to have up to 15 players on their roster but only 12 players are allowed to play in a game.
