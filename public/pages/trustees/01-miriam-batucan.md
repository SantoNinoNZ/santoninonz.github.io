Dame Miriam Batucan is the Founding Chair of the NZ-Filipino Sto. Niño Devotees Trust, established in 1994, and has been the driving force behind Sinulog NZ activities for more than 30 years.
An accountant by profession, Dame Miriam has worked with major multinational companies in New Zealand for the past three decades. Her dedication to faith, community service, and pastoral care has earned her numerous prestigious recognitions, including two Papal honors: the Benemerenti Medal, awarded by Pope Benedict XVI, and her investiture as a Dame of the Pontifical Equestrian Order of St. Sylvester Pope and Martyr.
She is also a recipient of an award in Clinical Pastoral Education and has devoted much of her life to pastoral care among both Filipino and non-Filipino communities. Her work has particularly focused on supporting young people, couples, and families across diverse communities.

In recognition of her outstanding voluntary service and commitment to community development, Dame Miriam was awarded the Queen's Service Medal (QSM) in the 2015 New Zealand Honours List.
Under her leadership, Sinulog NZ has also been appointed as a Community Connectors - Care in Community Welfare Provider, working in partnership with the Ministry of Social Development (MSD) and other government agencies to support and strengthen communities throughout New Zealand.

Message from Dame Miriam Batucan
"All glory and praise be to God for giving us the opportunity to help spread devotion to Señor Santo Niño here in Auckland, New Zealand. My heartfelt thanks go to all devotees and community groups who have worked hand in hand with us in keeping the devotion to Señor Santo Niño alive in this beautiful land of Aotearoa New Zealand.
Celebrating 30 years of faithful devotion to our beloved Señor Santo Niño is a tremendous blessing for all of us. Let us continue to nurture this devotion and pass it on to future generations. Together, may we keep the spirit of faith, unity, and service alive for many years to come.

Viva Señor Santo Niño! Pit Señor!"
